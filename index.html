Our Featured Product

SDAU Herbal Shampoo

Meet our Aloe Vera shampoo concept, developed as part of a student entrepreneurship project. The product brings together selected herbal ingredients and a ready-made shampoo base.

Designed around a nature-inspired identity, the project explores product presentation, branding and the basics of value addition.

A student initiative connecting agricultural education with product development and entrepreneurship.
